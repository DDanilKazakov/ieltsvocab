How progress works

Know increases mastery and schedules the item for a later review. Don't know lowers mastery and marks the item as Needs work. In Flashcards and Write you can switch to Difficult only to practise just those items. Two correct recalls in a row remove an item from the difficult list.

Progress is saved locally and, when you sign in, synced to your private Supabase account across devices.
